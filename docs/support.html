Getting started

How do I add a task quickly?

Type in the box at the top of My Day, or tap + (⌘N on Mac). Nexto understands natural language, so you can type everything in one line:

TypeResult
today, tomorrow, next friday, oct 12Schedules the task for that day
5pm, 9:30am, 17:00Sets a reminder time
due fridaySets a deadline
#errandsAdds a tag
@workMoves the task into a matching project
!, !!, !!!Low, medium or high priority
every day, every monday, monthlyRepeats the task
~30m, ~2hTime estimate, used by the Day Planner
somedayParks the task for later

Example: Pay rent every month due friday 9am #home !!

What's the difference between My Day and Lists?

My Day shows today: what's done, what's up next, your habits and your agenda. Lists holds everything else: Inbox, Upcoming, Priority, Backlog, Someday, Completed, plus your projects, areas and tags.

How do I add many tasks at once?

Use Brain Dump (the list icon on My Day, or ⇧⌘N on Mac). Paste or type one task per line:

  • A line ending in : or starting with # creates a project.
  • Indented lines become checklist items of the task above.
  • A blank line ends the current project.
  • Dates, tags, priority and estimates work on every line.

You see a preview before anything is added.

Planning

How does Auto-Plan work?

Auto-Plan places today's unscheduled tasks into free time within your working hours, most important first. It works around calendar events and existing blocks, and leaves a short break between blocks. A task's estimate sets its length (30 minutes if there's no estimate). Change your working hours and break length in Settings → Day Planner. Drag a block's handle to move it, or long-press it for more options.

My calendar events don't appear in the Planner.

Turn on Show Calendar Events in the Planner's ⋯ menu and allow calendar access. If you declined earlier, enable it in your device's Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars → Nexto (on Mac: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars). All-day events and events marked as "free" aren't shown.

How do repeating tasks work?

When you complete a repeating task, a copy is logged in Completed and the task moves to its next date with its checklist reset.

Habits and Focus

How do I check in a habit?

Tap the habit's ring on My Day, in Habits, or on the Habits widget. For goals with a count (like 8 glasses of water), each tap adds one. Once the goal is reached, tapping again resets the day. Long-press a habit for "Undo Check-In". To log a past day, open the habit and tap that day in the consistency map.

Why did my streak reset?

A streak counts scheduled days in a row on which you reached the goal. Days a habit isn't scheduled don't break it, and today doesn't break it until the day is over.

Does the focus timer keep running if I leave the app?

Yes. The timer keeps running, and you get a notification when the session ends. Time from completed focus sessions is added to the selected task.

Sync and devices

My tasks aren't syncing between devices.
  • Check that you're signed in to the same Apple Account on each device, with iCloud Drive turned on.
  • In Nexto, open Settings → Sync and check that iCloud Sync is on and shows On.
  • Check that you're online and not in Low Data or Low Power Mode, which can delay syncing.
  • Open Nexto on both devices and wait a minute. Changes sync in the background.
  • If you just changed the sync setting, quit and reopen Nexto.
How do I add the widgets?

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, and search for Nexto. Choose Today, Habits, or Up Next for the Lock Screen. You can complete tasks and check in habits right from the widget. On Mac, right-click the desktop and choose Edit Widgets.

I'm not getting reminders.

Open Settings → Reminders in Nexto. If notifications show as off, tap Open Settings and allow notifications for Nexto. Also check that Focus modes or Do Not Disturb aren't silencing Nexto.

Can I use Siri?

Yes. Say "Add a task in Nexto" or "What's left today in Nexto". Both are also available in the Shortcuts app.

Purchase

How much does Nexto cost?

Nexto is free to download with a 14-day free trial of every feature. After that, a one-time purchase of Nexto Lifetime ($29.99 in the US, local prices vary) unlocks everything forever. There's no subscription, and the trial never charges you automatically.

What happens when the trial ends?

Nothing is deleted. You can still open Nexto, see all your tasks and habits, complete tasks and check in habits. Creating new items and tools like Auto-Plan, Brain Dump and Focus ask you to unlock Nexto Lifetime.

Do I need to buy again on my iPad or Mac?

No. One purchase covers iPhone, iPad and Mac with the same Apple Account. On a new device, open Settings → Nexto Lifetime → Restore Purchases.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

How do I export or delete my data?

Settings → Export Data as JSON saves a copy of everything. Settings → Erase All Data deletes everything. With iCloud Sync on, this also erases the data on your other devices. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Email admin@vensofts.com. Screenshots help a lot. We read every message.
