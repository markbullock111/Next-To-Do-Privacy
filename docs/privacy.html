Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026 · Applies to Nexto for iPhone, iPad and Mac

In short: Nexto does not collect, store or share any personal data on our servers. We don't have servers for your data, and we don't use analytics, advertising or tracking. Your tasks, projects, habits and focus history stay on your devices and, if you turn on iCloud sync, in your own private iCloud account.

Who we are

Nexto is developed by Vensofts ("we", "us"). If you have questions about this policy, contact us at admin@vensofts.com.

Information we collect

None. Nexto has no user accounts and no sign-in. The app does not send your content or usage information to Vensofts or to any third party.

Where your data is stored

On your device

Everything you create in Nexto is stored locally on your device. This includes tasks, notes, checklists, projects, areas, tags, habits, check-ins and focus sessions. Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets read the same data through a storage area shared only between Nexto and its widgets on that device.

In your private iCloud (optional)

If iCloud Sync is on (Settings → Sync), Nexto uses Apple's CloudKit to sync your data between your own devices. The data is stored in the private database of your iCloud account. Vensofts cannot see, access or process it. Apple handles this storage under its own Privacy Policy. You can turn sync off at any time in Nexto's Settings or in your device's iCloud settings.

Permissions

PermissionWhy Nexto asksWhat happens to the data
Notifications Task reminders, habit reminders, the focus timer and the optional app icon badge. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Nothing is sent to us.
Calendars (optional) To show your calendar events in the Day Planner, so Auto-Plan can schedule around them. Events are read on your device only, never stored by Nexto and never uploaded.

You can change these permissions at any time in your device's Settings. Nexto works without them.

Siri, Shortcuts and widgets

When you add a task with Siri or Shortcuts, Apple processes the request as described in Apple's privacy policies, and Nexto saves the task on your device. Widgets show data from your device only.

Purchases

The free trial and Nexto Lifetime are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details. Nexto only checks with Apple, on your device, whether your Apple Account owns the trial or the lifetime unlock.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

Nexto contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code and no third-party SDKs. We do not track you across apps or websites. If you have opted in to share diagnostics with developers in your device settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports. These contain no personal content.

Exporting and deleting your data

  • Export: Settings → Export Data as JSON creates a file that you choose where to save or share.
  • Delete: Settings → Erase All Data deletes everything in Nexto. If iCloud Sync is on, this also removes the data from your other devices. Deleting the app removes its local data from that device.

Children

Nexto is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. If a change affects how your data is handled, we will describe it in the app's release notes.
